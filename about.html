About us

Who we are and why one shared chart matters.

Our story

A shared chart, built on a simple idea.

Heal Yeah! began with one clinic and one frustration: patients kept repeating their history to every new clinician. We connected our facilities around a single record so care could follow the patient.

Today four facilities, six departments, and a triage nurse line work from the same chart.
