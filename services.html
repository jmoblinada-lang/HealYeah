Services

Three ways in, so you get the right care at the right speed.

Services

Three doors in. Here's how to pick the right one.

When in doubt, call — a triage nurse can route you in under two minutes.

Scheduled

Primary & specialty care

For anything that can wait a few days and benefits from a clinician who knows your history.

  • Annual physicals and screenings
  • Chronic condition check-ins
  • Referrals to specialists
  • Medication management
  • Preventive care and wellness visits
Same-day

Urgent care

For issues that need attention today but aren't life-threatening.

  • Infections, sprains, minor fractures
  • Fevers that won't come down
  • Cuts that may need stitches
  • Severe sore throats or earaches
  • Minor burns or allergic reactions
Immediate

Emergency room

For anything that could be life-threatening. Don't wait — call your local emergency number.

  • Chest pain or difficulty breathing
  • Signs of stroke
  • Severe bleeding or major trauma
  • Loss of consciousness or seizures
  • Sudden severe confusion or weakness

How it works

From request to visit in four steps.

1
