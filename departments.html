Departments

Every specialty, one shared chart.

A referral between departments carries your full history with it — no re-explaining, no repeat scans.

Primary Care

Annual physicals, screenings, and a clinician who tracks your baseline over years.

Cardiology

Risk assessment, monitoring, and follow-up for heart and blood pressure conditions.

Pediatrics

Well-child visits, vaccination schedules, and growth tracking from birth on.

Dermatology

Skin checks, chronic skin conditions, and minor in-office procedures.

Physical therapy

Post-injury and post-surgical recovery plans built around your goals.

Behavioral health

Counseling and psychiatric care, coordinated with your primary clinician.
